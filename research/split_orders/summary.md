# Leader orders split over time: the Hyperliquid price path from their own fills

Generated by `uv run python scripts/split_orders.py` (2026-11-21). Development data only (fills before
2025-12-03); taker orders of eligible core perps; forced fills (ADL, liquidation, settlement) excluded. Outputs in
this folder: `prevalence.csv`, `path_by_elapsed.csv`, `second_child_by_size.csv`, `children.parquet` (every later
child order with `src_row` and `oid`).

**Question.** A copy arrives after the leader's order: does the price at that moment match what the replay assumes
(base: the leader order's VWAP plus drift; stress: its deepest fill plus drift)? The leader's own next order in the
same direction shows the Hyperliquid price at a known time after the first one: its best (first) fill is the top of
the book when it arrived.

**Facts.**
- All 114,364 taker orders fill within one millisecond: no order executes over time, so a copy always arrives after
  the leader's whole sweep.
- A "split trade" is consecutive taker orders by the same address, coin and side, at most 60 s apart. 6.4% of
  sequences on BTC/ETH/HYPE/SOL and 7.7% on other coins are split (median 2 orders), but they carry 16.9% and 25.4%
  of the notional.

**Reading.**
- At the measured copy latency (about 1-2 s) the next price sits at about the leader's VWAP on the main coins
  (+0.2 to +0.3 bps) and between the VWAP and the deepest fill on other coins: the book has not fully refilled.
  The replay's base anchor (VWAP) matches on the main coins and is about right on other coins at 1-2 s; within 1 s
  on other coins the deepest fill (the stress anchor) is closer. Samples under 2 s are small (300 orders on other
  coins).
- Medians are about zero at every horizon: the typical next order gets the same price. The means come from a minority
  of large moves (90th percentile 20-60 bps on other coins), so the cost is in the tail.
- Over 5-30 s on other coins, prices keep moving in the leader's direction by several bps beyond the VWAP, and small
  first orders (<1k) are followed by worse prices (+8 bps): leaders who split are often chasing a move. This is
  conditional on the leader placing another order, so it is not an unconditional drift estimate.
- Conclusion: no change to the replay's anchors. The base anchor is consistent with the venue's own prices at the
  measured latency on the main coins and close on the rest; the stress anchor (deepest fill) covers the sub-second
  case on thin coins. The tail on other coins supports keeping the conservative alt drift bound (ADR-0114).
